# Samsung Galaxy XR Pitch Concept

## Concept

Render Arena scenes can become immersive spatial canvases for Samsung Galaxy XR viewers.

## Why Samsung Galaxy XR

- accessible XR pathway
- rich visual scenes
- potential mainstream adoption
- natural fit for HomelandAI scene-based storytelling

## Experience

Viewers can:

- examine floating render canvases
- compare variants spatially
- explore Render Vault rooms
- experience Homeland Safari environments
- watch guest reactions live

## Strategic value

HomelandAI can help make XR feel practical, entertaining, and visually desirable.
