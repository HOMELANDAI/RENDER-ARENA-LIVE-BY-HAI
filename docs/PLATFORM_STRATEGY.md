# Platform Strategy

Render Arena is designed for three primary platforms, each serving a different role.

## Twitch

Twitch is the live energy platform.

Best for:

- viewer reactions
- fast chat feedback
- live voting
- clip moments
- creator community

## Maestro.tv

Maestro is the premium structured-interaction platform.

Best for:

- live modules
- polls
- gated content
- curated replays
- premium community experiences

## YouTube

YouTube is the evergreen archive and discovery platform.

Best for:

- full episodes
- highlight reels
- shorts
- educational prompt breakdowns
- behind-the-scenes documentaries

## Strategy

Each live episode should create multiple assets:

- full replay
- YouTube episode
- short clips
- prompt breakdown
- Render Vault entry
- platform teaser
- possible sponsor proof-of-performance segment
