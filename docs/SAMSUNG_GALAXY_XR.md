# Samsung Galaxy XR Strategy

## Role

Samsung Galaxy XR is positioned as an accessible immersive viewing pathway for Render Arena's future spatial experiences.

## Why it fits HomelandAI

- richer scene-driven presentation
- more accessible price point than some premium XR alternatives
- Android ecosystem potential
- better alignment with mainstream adoption goals

## Potential experiences

### Spatial Canvas

Generated images appear as floating canvases in the viewer's physical space.

### Layered Scene View

Background, midground, foreground, character, and effects layers exist at different depths.

### Virtual Render Vault

Viewers walk through archived renders as a gallery.

### Guest Immersion Mode

A live guest experiences the render inside the headset while hosts and audience watch their reaction.

## Recommendation

Develop after the 2D livestream workflow has strong audience response.
