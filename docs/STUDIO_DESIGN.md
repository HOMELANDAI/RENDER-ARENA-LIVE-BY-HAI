# Studio Design

## Core layout

Two studious Black male hosts sit at opposing minimalist desks facing each other across the studio. The setup signals dialogue, strategy, and creative tension.

## Desk system

Each desk includes:

- 24-inch bright portable OLED display
- wireless LED keyboard
- wireless LED mouse
- matte black desk surface
- HAI engraving on the front or edge
- subtle under-lighting

## Workstations

Two custom glowing gaming towers sit on the floor against the desks.

### Tower A

- angular vertical profile
- smoked or frosted glass
- warm amber internal lighting
- exposed structural elements

### Tower B

- sculptural or geometric body
- matte black or dark mesh
- cool blue-white internal glow
- distinct from Tower A

## Screen wall

Three large horizontal screens:

- Left: HAI Colorway system and palette cards
- Center: main live render or final reveal
- Right: prompt logic, parameters, iterations, and render notes

## Branding

- Neon HAI typography beneath the screen wall
- HAI engravings on desks
- subtle signage rather than excessive branding

## Design tone

- deep charcoal and black
- old gold accents
- controlled amber and cool white lighting
- cinematic broadcast atmosphere
- no clutter
