# Character Bible

## Purpose

The Character Bible protects continuity across Homeland Safari episodes.

## Required fields per explorer

- name
- breed
- role
- silhouette
- clothing
- gear
- personality
- recurring props
- HOMELANDAI mark placement

## Canon rules

- adult anthropomorphic canine explorers
- professional expedition clothing
- realistic gear
- subtle HAI branding
- consistent role identity
- no random costume changes without story reason
