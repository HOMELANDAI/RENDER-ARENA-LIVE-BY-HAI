# Studio Prompts

## Opposing Minds OLED Edition

A cinematic studio interior designed for a premium AI livestream show. Two studious Black men sit at opposing minimalist desks, facing each other across the room. Each desk features a 24-inch ultra-bright portable OLED display, wireless LED keyboard and mouse, and a custom glowing gaming tower on the floor. Matte black desks have HAI engraved into the front face. Behind them are three large wall-mounted screens: colorway grid, active AI render, and prompt parameters. Beneath the screens, thin neon HAI signage glows softly. Moody professional lighting, cool whites, soft ambers, cinematic 8K realism.

## Strategic Exchange Dual Tower Focus

A high-end creative studio set designed for a live AI art show. Two focused Black men sit at facing desks, slightly angled toward one another. No laptops, only 24-inch portable OLED monitors, wireless LED keyboards and mice. Distinct custom gaming towers sit below each desk: one monolithic black with gold light channels, one geometric with blue-white glow. Three massive wall screens show final render, prompt structure, and colorway variants. Neon HAI typography runs horizontally under the screens.

## Command and Control

A premium AI broadcast studio designed as a modern creative command center. Two composed Black men sit at opposing desks with OLED displays, wireless peripherals, and contrasting custom gaming towers. HAI engravings are backlit on desk fronts. The back wall features three screens: dominant AI artwork, palette cards, prompt logic. Thin neon HAI emblem beneath screens. Restrained luxury and cinematic lighting.
