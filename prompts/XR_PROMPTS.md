# XR Prompts

## Spatial Canvas System

Generated AI images materialize as floating 3D canvases in the viewer's space. Main generation appears in center field of view. Previous iterations float at the sides for comparison. Colorway palette cards are interactive spatial objects viewers can inspect. Host feeds appear as if in the room.

## AI Generation in Spatial Layers

Break AI generations into compositional layers: background, midground, foreground, character, and effects. Display each layer at different spatial depth to create parallax as viewers move their heads.

## Virtual Gallery Space

Create a persistent virtual gallery where all generated content from episodes exists in 3D space. Each colorway has its own room or section. Viewers can walk through, examine details, and vote on candidates.

## Real-Time 2D-to-3D Generation

Winning 2D renders are passed into a 3D generation system. XR viewers see the resulting model appear as an object in space and walk around it.
