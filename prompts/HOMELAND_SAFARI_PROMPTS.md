# Homeland Safari Prompt Framework

## Expedition sequence prompt structure

Create a cinematic stylized 3D animated expedition scene with seven adult anthropomorphic canine explorers wearing professional expedition clothing. The setting is a remote archaeological environment with realistic wildlife, tactile analog gear, subtle HOMELANDAI branding, and reverent archaeology tone.

## Phase prompts

1. Route: team approaches through terrain.
2. Survey: maps, compasses, and field notes define site.
3. Excavation: ropes, markers, tools, dirt, weathering, and pit depth evolve logically.
4. Reveal: artifact edges appear gradually.
5. Documentation: sketches, photos, notes, and measurements.
6. Canon expansion: artifact connects to larger mythology.

## Continuity rules

- clothing gets dirtier over time
- weather changes gradually
- gear remains consistent
- artifact exposure progresses logically
- no sudden full reveals
- brand marks stay subtle
