# HAI Colorway System

HAI colorways are not only colors. They define mood, material, lighting, environment, and narrative tone.

## Core colorways

### Carbon Slice Supreme

Dark metallic surface, teal trace lines, carbon texture, precision and performance.

### HAI Jacq Supreme

Jewel violet, radial glow, premium fabric feel, mysterious and luxurious.

### Sage Variant

Earth green, organic textures, expedition tone, natural and grounded.

### Vice Edition

Ultraviolet-black, neon edge, nightlife energy, cyber atmosphere.

### Old Gold Legacy

Antique gold, maroon depth, heritage, prestige, archive quality.

### Render Teal Core

Teal-blue technical glow, workstation energy, AI command center tone.

- prompt constraints

- Render Vault filters
