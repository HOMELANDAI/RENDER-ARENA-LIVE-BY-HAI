# Brand Guidelines

## Brand names

- HomelandAI
- HAI
- Render Arena Live by HAI

## Brand principle

Premium creative intelligence. Structured visual imagination. Scene-first storytelling.

## Tone

- cinematic
- precise
- confident
- premium
- technical
- visionary

## Avoid

- generic AI startup language
- chaotic prompt language
- over-bright gaming visuals
- cheap neon overload

## Approved language

- Every render has a story.
- Every story has a world.
- Prompt. Render. Refine. Reveal.
- The arena where ideas become worlds.
- Built for live creation, structured interaction, and immersive futures.
